# Ripple Plan

## MVP

- Import transactions from the Nessie API.
- Show each purchase's cost in dollars now, 30-year future value, and carbon impact.
- Replay the long-term effect of purchases over 30 years.
- Use SerpApi to suggest cheaper or greener alternatives.

## Stretch goals

- Scan receipts to capture purchases.
- Add a shared household shopping list.
- Simulate nudges and their effects on spending and carbon.

## Team split

- **Frontend:** Dashboard, purchase breakdown, and replay experience.
- **Backend + data:** FastAPI, Nessie integration, models, and synthetic seed data.
- **AI + search:** Carbon estimates, LLM workflow, and SerpApi alternatives.
- **Product + demo:** User story, visual direction, pitch, and demo flow.
